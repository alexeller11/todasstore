# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone. We will not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or any behavior that makes contributors feel unsafe.

## Expected Behavior
- Use respectful language.
- Be open to constructive criticism.
- Give credit where credit is due.
- Encourage collaboration.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Disrespectful or offensive language.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Violations may result in removal from the project community.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
